Our 1-Year Technical Evolution

Comprehensive breakdown of our major core software solutions deployed over the past year

1 Year Ago Milestone

QR-Based Attendance & School Management System

Designed and built to completely eliminate manual register keeping and proxy entries in schools. This institutional utility streamlined day-to-day operations by issuing unique secure QR identification tags to every student, turning roll-call into a frictionless digital event.

Rapid Contactless Scanning

Uses camera-based web or mobile scanner modules to instantly decode encrypted student attendance markers in under 0.5 seconds per head.

Automated Daily Logs

Instantly records timestamp, attendance status (Present/Absent/Late), and class identifiers directly to a centralized local database storage ledger.

Administrative Insights

Generates instant monthly percentage summaries, notifies parents or school admins regarding chronic absenteeism, and exports reports easily.

Current Flagship Milestone

AI Learning Platform & Cognitive Engine

Our current state-of-the-art educational application designed to simulate personal tutoring at scale. It incorporates speech processing, dynamic doubt resolution, custom PDF study notes parsing, and local browser persistence.
